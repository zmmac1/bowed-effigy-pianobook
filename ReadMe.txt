## Release notes

Bowed Effigy is an instrument from a lost civilization. It's a primitive, bowed string instrument with very tenuous tuning and intonation. Despite that, it offers a truly unique timbre when compared to modern string instruments. Somewhere between a viola and cello, it is soulful, noisy and otherworldly.

The DecentSampler version requires the instrument be loaded into RAM and not streamed from disk. I don't know why this works on Kontakt and not DS, but I experienced dropouts and artifacts when trying to stream with Decent Sampler. I also recommend increasing your max voice count when using any of the ensemble patches.

## Using the instrument

KISS (Keep It Simple Stupid) was the name of the game. There are a bunch of presets whose names hopefully describe their intended function. For controls, you have mic balance control over two stereo setups, a mid-side close mic'ing of the instrument and a more distant omni pair.

Legato is handled differently for each platform. Legato patches for Kontakt are separate presets which make use of the WIPS library. This is an amazing script by Big Bob and Nils from a long time ago that still crushes. If you want to adjust the vibrato, hit that separate tab at the bottom of the Kontakt legato patches.

Legato on DecentSampler is scripted by me and not as good, but is still functional. The toggle at the bottom of the interface turns it on/off where available.

Knobs:

Body - controls both a highpass and lowpass filter to control instrument frequency response as desired.

Grain(Kontakt-only) - a subtle distortion that adds additional bite as needed.

Vibrato (DecentSampler-only) - artificial vibrato to give it some depth. To the left is slow and shallow, to the right is fast and deep.

Drift - A wow and flutter effect. Controls are similar, but each platform is slightly different.

Pressure - Controls velocity -> amplitude modulation. To the left, all samples play at the same volume. To the right, dynamics are fully at your fingertips.

Air - Reverb return amount.

NOTE: Extended Reverb and Drift controls are accessible by clicking the labels for each control on the main display.

## The Story

I made this instrument for myself mainly. I was working on a cue for a period drama and wanted something like a cello, but more fragile and inconsistent. I took a nod from another Pianobook instrument, Stringbow by Northr, and decided to expand on his concept. Bowed Effigy is a steel string acoustic guitar, bowed like a cello. It offers a ton of variety and can be used as a solo instrument or as an ensemble. Great for drones, melodies and evolving chords. I have included some custom programmed pads too for additional fun.
